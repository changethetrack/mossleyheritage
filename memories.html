Share your Mossley memories

Mossley Civic Society is collecting stories, photographs and recordings for the Heritage Centre’s new digital archive. Whether you grew up here or came to Mossley later, we’d love to hear from you.

A few lines is plenty. If you’re sharing a memory, tell us where and roughly when it happened if you remember. If you have a photo, letter or item, you can describe it here. If you’d prefer a chat, just let us know.

Please leave one if you’d like a reply or want to arrange a recorded chat.

We’ll ask your permission before including your contribution in the digital archive.

You’re also welcome to visit the Heritage Centre to share a memory or show us a photograph or item in person.
